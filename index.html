Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical kitchen remodel take?
A standard kitchen remodel typically takes between 6 to 12 weeks, depending on the scope of work, material availability, and structural changes required.
Do you provide free estimates?
Yes, we provide a complimentary initial consultation and a high-level estimate. Detailed architectural planning and fixed quotes are provided during the design phase.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured in accordance with state regulations to ensure our clients and our workers are protected.
Can I stay in my home during the renovation?
In many cases, yes. We take great care to minimize disruption and maintain a clean workspace. However, for full home renovations, we may suggest temporary relocation for your comfort.
